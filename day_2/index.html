Welcome all to the Haldia Institute of Technology

Haldia Institute of Technology (HIT) is one of the leading private engineering institutes in West Bengal, begin your journey toward a successful and rewarding career in engineering and technology with HIT.

Haldia Institute of Technology – A Leading Engineering Institute at Haldia, Dist-Purba Medinipur, WestBengal

Haldia Institute of Technology was established to deliver quality technical education and has been offering undergraduate and postgraduate Engineering and Management programmes affiliated to Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), West Bengal - formerly known as West Bengal University of Technology and approved by AICTE, Government of India.
